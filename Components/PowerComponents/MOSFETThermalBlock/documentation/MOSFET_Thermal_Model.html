Description

The MOSFET Model block models an ideal switching MOSFET with a thermal port. Conduction loss, switching loss and body diode loss are evaluated in Simulink. The total loss is injected as heat into a junction thermal mass. The junction connects through the junction to case thermal resistance to port A, and the junction temperature feeds back into the loss tables.

Switching loss is taken from turn-on and turn-off energy tables, scaled by the voltage and current latched at each commutation. A commutation into an already conducting body diode is treated as soft switching, so no switching energy is charged for it.

The gate is a physical port. Drive it with a controlled voltage source, or with the gate voltage levels a PWM generator block produces, and set the gate-source threshold to a value between the OFF and ON levels.
