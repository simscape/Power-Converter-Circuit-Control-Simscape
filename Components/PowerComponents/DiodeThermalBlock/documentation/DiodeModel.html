Description

The Diode Model block models a diode with a thermal port. The electrical behavior is simple-fidelity. Conduction loss is evaluated from the tabulated forward I-V curve and reverse recovery loss from the tabulated recovery energy, both in Simulink. The total loss is injected as heat into a junction thermal mass. The junction connects through the junction to case thermal resistance to port A, and the junction temperature feeds back into the loss tables.

Both loss tables are interpolated on the junction temperature, so the device conducts at the temperature it reaches rather than at a fixed temperature.

Ports

Conserving Ports

PortDescription
+Anode.
-Cathode.
AAmbient, or case, thermal port.
Note: Port A is a thermal conserving port. Connect it to a Thermal Reference block, a Temperature Source block or a thermal network. The junction temperature is computed inside the block and is not exposed as a port.
