Full Bridge PWM Generator

Four-element bridge gate vector with dead time, from a duty cycle

Description

The Full Bridge PWM Generator block turns a commanded duty cycle into the four gate signals of one full bridge. The leg pair is produced as in the Half Bridge PWM Generator block and is then mapped onto the two legs, so the bridge switches as two diagonals.

The commanded duty cycle is limited to Tdead*Fsw to 1 - Tdead*Fsw, so the dead time always fits inside the period and the two devices of a leg are never on together.

Ports

Input

PortSizeDescription
duty1Commanded duty cycle of the first diagonal, 0 to 1.

Output

PortSizeDescription
G4Gate vector for the four devices of the bridge.

Gate Vector Order

ElementDrivesRole
1Leg A high-side deviceFirst diagonal.
2Leg A low-side deviceSecond diagonal.
3Leg B high-side deviceSecond diagonal.
4Leg B low-side deviceFirst diagonal.
Note: Elements 1 and 4 form one conducting diagonal and elements 2 and 3 the other. Elements 1 and 2 are the two devices of one leg, so they are never on together.
Note: The output is a gate voltage, not a logic level. Set gateVoltageOn and gateVoltageOff to straddle the gate threshold of the device you drive, and drive a Simscape™ gate through a controlled voltage source.

Model Options

solverMode - Solver mode

OptionEffect
Variable-step (continuous)The internal timer is an integrator. Use it with a continuous or a variable-step solver.
Fixed-step (discrete)The internal timer is a discrete counter running at Ts. Use it with a fixed-step solver, or to keep the gate edges on a known grid.
