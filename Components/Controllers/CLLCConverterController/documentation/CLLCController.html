CLLC Controller

Frequency control with synchronous rectification for a bidirectional CLLC converter

Description

The CLLC Controller block controls the CLLC Converter plant by varying the switching frequency. A voltage or current loop produces a current error, the compensator turns it into a control voltage, and the voltage-controlled oscillator turns that into a normalized frequency between Fmin and Fmax. A CLLC PWM Generator block then produces the two gate vectors.

The mode input sets the direction of power flow. It picks the current the loop regulates and it swaps the two gate vectors, so the driven bridge and the synchronously rectifying bridge exchange roles without any other change.

Gate Vector Order

Both vectors use the same element order, and it matches the plant inputs Gp and Gs.

ElementDrivesRole
1Leg A high-side deviceFirst diagonal.
2Leg A low-side deviceSecond diagonal.
3Leg B high-side deviceSecond diagonal.
4Leg B low-side deviceFirst diagonal.
Note: Elements 1 and 4 form one conducting diagonal and elements 2 and 3 the other. Elements 1 and 2 are the two devices of one leg, so they are never on together.

Model Options

controllerDomain - Controller time domain

The time domain applies to the whole controller. It sets the form of the voltage loop, the form of the compensator that cntrlSelect names, and the carrier the PWM generator uses.

OptionEffect
ContinuousThe voltage loop and the compensator are continuous-time blocks, and the PWM generator uses the variable-step carrier.
DiscreteThe voltage loop and the compensator are discrete-time blocks, the voltage loop at TsVoltage and the compensator at TsCurrent, and the PWM generator uses the fixed-step carrier. TsVoltage, TsCurrent and pwmSampleTime become visible.

cntrlSelect - Controller type

OptionEffect
2P2Z compensatorA two-pole two-zero transfer function scaled by Kc. The coefficients are numSdomain and denSdomain in the continuous domain, and numZdomain and denZdomain in the discrete domain.
PI controllerA PI on Kp and Ki, in the domain controllerDomain names.

vcoSelect - Voltage-controlled oscillator type

OptionEffect
Constant rateOne gain, vcoGain, over the whole frequency range.
Two rateTwo gains, vcoGainVec, so the frequency moves at a different rate on each side of resonance.
Note: pwmSampleTime reaches the CLLC PWM Generator block inside this controller. Set it on this dialog box rather than on the generator. The carrier implementation comes from controllerDomain, so the generator has no solver mode of its own here.
