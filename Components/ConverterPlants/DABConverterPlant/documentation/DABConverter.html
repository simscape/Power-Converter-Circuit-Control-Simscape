Description

The DAB Converter block models a dual active bridge DC-DC power stage: two full bridges coupled by a high-frequency transformer with a series inductance. Two independent four-element gate vectors drive the two bridges, and the phase shift between them sets the direction and the amount of power transferred.

The circuit contains:

  • Primary bridge - four switches, A1, A2, B1 and B2.
  • Series inductance Ls, the energy transfer element.
  • High-frequency transformer, with its turns ratio and winding resistances.
  • Secondary bridge - four switches, A1, A2, B1 and B2.
  • Output filter - the capacitor Co with its series resistance.

Gate Vector Order

Both vectors use the same element order, and it is the same order in both model options.

ElementDrivesRole
Gp(1), Gs(1)MOSFETPriA1, MOSFETSecA1Leg A high side.
Gp(2), Gs(2)MOSFETPriA2, MOSFETSecA2Leg A low side.
Gp(3), Gs(3)MOSFETPriB1, MOSFETSecB1Leg B high side.
Gp(4), Gs(4)MOSFETPriB2, MOSFETSecB2Leg B low side.
Note: Elements 1 and 4 form one diagonal of the bridge and elements 2 and 3 form the other, so a square wave across the transformer needs 1 and 4 driven together, then 2 and 3. The DAB PWM Generator block produces both vectors in this order.

Export to Unmasked Subsystem

The Export to Unmasked Subsystem button writes the DAB Converter circuit to a new model with no mask, alongside a parameter file that reproduces every mask value. Use it to inspect or edit the circuit beyond what the mask exposes.

File written to the current folderContents
DAB_exported_<timestamp>.slxThe unmasked circuit. Its PreLoadFcn runs the parameter file.
DAB_exported_<timestamp>_params.mAssigns the structure DAB in the base workspace. Every block parameter of the exported model references a field of this structure.
Note: The export reduces each variant subsystem to its active choice, so the exported model holds only the device choice the mask had when you exported it.

Plant Test Harness

The DAB Converter block has an open-loop test harness, DABTestHarnessPlant.slx, in the test folder of DABConverterPlant. The harness holds this block, the PWM generator blocks that drive its gate port, the input source, the load and a scope on the measurement bus. It carries no controller: constant reference blocks set the operating point, so the harness exercises the power stage on its own.

BlockRole in the harness
DAB ConverterThis block, taken from ConverterPlantLibrary, at the mask values the shipped converter model uses.
DAB PWM GeneratorDAB PWM Generator from ConverterLibrary, which drives both Gp and Gs. Switching frequency and dead time from DABDesign, gate levels from DABController.gateVoltage.
Phase Shift ReferenceConstant DABDesign.dutyRatedNominal*pi. The generator takes the bridge-to-bridge phase shift in radians, and the design stores the rated shift as a fraction of pi.
Duty ReferenceConstant 0.5, a square wave on each bridge leg.
Vin, RloadDC source at DABDesign.V1nom and load DABDesign.V2nom^2/DABDesign.ratedPower. The rated phase shift transfers the rated power only into the rated load, so both come from the design rather than from the defaults script.
Subsystem, Mux, ScopeThe measurement subsystem of the converter model, unchanged, which forms the output voltage, current and power from the measurement bus.

Opening the harness runs DABDefaultInputParameters through its PreLoadFcn, so every design and controller structure the generator masks read is in the base workspace before the first simulation. The solver is ode23t, as in the converter model the harness was taken from.

The stop time is 15 ms.
