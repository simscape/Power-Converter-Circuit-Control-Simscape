Power Converter Utilities

Functions that simulate and analyze the converter models

PowerConverterUtilities

These functions simulate a converter model and analyze its waveforms, power loss, efficiency and harmonics. They also select catalog parts and apply them to the plant. One set of functions serves every converter in this project: each function finds the plant, its device options and its operating-point blocks through converterRegistry.
