Power Converter Component Library

List of Components

The table lists the custom components in this project: the power stages and controllers of the resonant, power factor correction and non-isolated DC-DC converters, the grid-forming inverter controller, the PWM generators, and the semiconductor device models they use. The library ConverterLibrary, Components/ConverterLibrary.slx, holds every component listed below.

To open the documentation of a component, which the Help button on the block mask also opens, click its Documentation link. To open its test model, click its Test Harness link.
